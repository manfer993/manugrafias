Privacy Policy - ManuGrafias AR

ManuGrafias AR lets you see videos over printed photographs using your phone's camera.

Camera

The camera is used only to recognize the photograph in front of you. The images are processed on your device and are never recorded, stored or sent to any server.

Personal data

We do not ask for, collect or store any personal data. You do not need an account.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not use cookies, analytics or any tracking tools.

Hosting

Our hosting providers (GitHub and Google Firebase) may log technical data such as IP addresses for security purposes. We do not use this data to identify you.

External links

The "Visit Website" button opens the photographer's website, which has its own privacy policy.
